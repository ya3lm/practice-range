# Shot list

Recording: 1920×1080 → crop to 1080×1080 (LinkedIn feed) with the crosshair centred. 60 fps capture, export 30 fps. Slow-mo is in-engine (Time.timeScale), not post.
Captions: burned in, top third, white on 60% black band, 36 px, max 6 words. First caption at 0.0 s.
Palette per mode is in `ModeDefaults`. Keep the target colour at #00FFFF for mid, #FF8C1A for close, #A6FF33 for far so the series is recognisable.

Per-clip checklist:
- [ ] first frame is motion, not a title card
- [ ] engineering caption at 0.0 s
- [ ] no gun model in frame, no face, no blood
- [ ] ends on chart / limitation / diagram, held 3 s
- [ ] muted test: does it make sense with sound off?
- [ ] ≤45 s
